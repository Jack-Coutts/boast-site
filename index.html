How it works

From the court to your stats in seconds.

  1. Log the match

    Pick who you played and type the scores. It takes seconds.

  2. Read your game plan

    Before you play, check your tactics and your record against them.

  3. Know your opponent

    Note their strengths and weaknesses, and see which kinds of match you win.

  4. See your season

    Browse every match as a list or a calendar, and spot your form.

Private by design

Your matches stay on your phone.

No account, nothing public

boast isn't a ratings site. Open the app and start logging, with nothing to sign up for and nothing posted anywhere.

No ads, no tracking

boast collects nothing. Your notes and game plans are yours alone.

Yours to keep

Back up everything whenever you like, and take it to a new phone.
