Privacy policy

Last updated 6 October 2026

boast is a squash match log. It's designed so your data stays yours.

What boast collects

Nothing. boast has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer receives no data about you or how you use the app.

Where your data lives

Your matches, opponents, game plans, notes and settings are stored only on your device. Your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) may include them, under the terms of that service.

Sharing

Data leaves your device only when you choose to send it:

  • Share score and Share stats create an image of a result card. Cards never include your notes, tags, game plans, strengths or weaknesses.
  • Share history creates a file with match dates, formats and scores against one opponent. It never includes private fields.
  • Export backup creates a file of everything, including private notes, for you to keep somewhere safe.

In each case your device's share sheet sends the file, and you choose where it goes.

Children

boast doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that sends data off your device, such as optional sync, this policy will be updated before that version is released and will say exactly what is sent and why.
