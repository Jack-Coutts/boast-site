Getting started

The first time you open boast, it asks your name. Your name goes on the results you share, so a card reads "Jack beat Sam" rather than "Me beat Sam". You can skip this and add it later in Settings.

boast has two tabs. Matches lists every match you've logged. Opponents lists the people you play. The gear button opens Settings.

Logging a match

  1. Tap Log match on the Matches tab. To log a match against someone you're already looking at, tap + on their page instead.
  2. Pick who you played from the suggestions, or type a name. A new name adds a new opponent when you save.
  3. Check the format under their name. It starts as whatever you last played against them. Tap it to change it.
  4. Type the scores, yours on the left. The cursor moves on as soon as a score is complete, so 11–7 is just 1, 1, 7. A score that could still grow, such as 1 (could be 10 to 19), waits: tap Next above the keyboard to move on.
  5. Optionally add tags (such as physical or fast-paced), a note, or a different date. Tap Today to choose yesterday, an earlier day this week, or any other date.
  6. Tap Save.

To remove a game you added by mistake, press and hold its number. If you tap Cancel after changing anything, boast asks before discarding it.

boast warns you about unusual scores, such as 11–10 with no two-point lead, but never stops you saving. Friendlies get played to all sorts of rules.

Formats and scoring

  • PAR 11 or PAR 15, best of 3 or 5. Point-a-rally to 11 or 15, win by two.
  • Hi-ho, best of 3 or 5. Traditional scoring to 9. At 8–8 the receiver chooses to play to 9 or 10, so 9–8, 10–8 and 10–9 are all valid finishes.
  • Fixed games, 1 to 7 games, to 11 or 15. For sessions where you play a set number of games whatever happens. These matches can be drawn.

A game counts as won once someone reaches the target score (11, 15 or 9). A best-of match is won by the first player to take the majority of games. Change the format you start with in Settings → Default format.

Extra and unfinished games

Extra games. Once a match is decided, boast closes the keyboard. If you keep playing, tap Add extra game. Extra games are saved and shown separately, but they never change the result or your stats.

Unfinished matches. If you stop before the match is decided, for example 11–7, 11–5, 6–4 when the court time runs out, boast saves it as Unfinished. A game that stopped before anyone reached the target, like that 6–4, doesn't count as won. Unfinished matches stay in your history but aren't counted in your record.

A match

Tap any match to open it. At the top is its card: date, who won, the score of every game, any extra games and the format.

  • Share score sends the card as an image. See Sharing results.
  • Your tags and note appear below the card. They're private and never appear on the card.
  • Edit reopens the match to change anything. Delete removes it after asking.

Opponents

The Opponents tab lists everyone you've played, most recent first, with your recent form and record against each.

  • Add an opponent before you've played them by typing a name in Add opponent at the end of the list. Their page opens so you can write a game plan.
  • Rename an opponent with Name at the bottom of their page. Every match against them updates.
  • Delete an opponent with Delete opponent at the bottom of their page. boast asks first, and also deletes your matches against them.

Game plans and scouting

Each opponent's page opens with their game plan: a short numbered list of tactics to read before you walk on court.

  • Type in Add tactic and tap Done to add a line. Tap a line to edit it. Press and hold a line to edit or delete it.
  • Further down, add their strengths and weaknesses. As you type, boast suggests ones you've used for other opponents. Tap × on one to remove it.

Game plans, strengths and weaknesses are private. They never appear on shared cards or in shared history.

Your record and stats

  • Overall. The top of the Matches tab shows your record and your last five results, oldest first: W for a win, L for a loss, D for a draw.
  • Head to head. Each opponent's page has a card with your record against them, the period it covers, your recent form (last five), win rate, and games won and lost. Tap Share stats to send it as an image.
  • Each match in an opponent's list also shows its points difference, such as +10.

Only the games that decided a match count. Extra games and unfinished matches are left out of every stat.

The calendar

On the Matches tab, tap the calendar button beside the gear to switch between the list and a month calendar. Tap it again to go back to the list.

  • Days you played are in bold with a dot underneath: black if you won a match that day, grey otherwise. Today has a ring.
  • Tap a day to see its matches below. Use the arrows to change month.

Sharing results

Match cards and head-to-head cards are designed to be shared. Share score or Share stats turns the card into an image and opens your phone's share sheet, so you can send it in Messages, WhatsApp or anywhere else. Cards show names, dates and scores only. Tags, notes, game plans, strengths and weaknesses are never included.

Two kinds of file

boast makes two kinds of file, for two different jobs.

  • A backup is for you. It holds everything in your boast and moves it to another phone. See Backups and new phones.
  • A shared history is for the person you played. It holds your matches against them, so they don't have to log them again. See Sending history to a friend.

Both are plain JSON text files, named like boast-backup-2026-10-06.json and boast-history-sam.json. You open either one with Settings → Import, and boast tells you which kind it is.

Sending history to a friend

Say you're Jack and you play Sam, who also uses boast. You can send Sam every match you've logged against them.

  1. Make sure your name is set in Settings. The file uses it to say who it's from.
  2. Open Sam's page, tap Send history to Sam and send the file to Sam.
  3. Sam opens boast, taps Settings → Import and chooses the file. boast says "Jack shared 5 matches against Sam" and asks Sam to confirm with I'm Sam.
  4. boast then asks who Jack is in Sam's boast. If Sam already has an opponent called Jack, that opponent is at the top, so it's one tap. Otherwise Sam can add Jack as a new opponent.

Sam's copy of boast flips every score to Sam's side, so your 11–7 becomes their 7–11, and skips matches Sam has already logged. The file contains dates, formats and scores only, never notes, tags or game plans.

If you open a shared history that isn't meant for you, tap This isn't me and nothing changes. To move your own matches, use a backup instead.

Backups and new phones

All your data lives on your phone. To keep your own copy, or to move to a new phone:

  1. On your old phone, tap Settings → Export backup and save the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone, such as Files or an email to yourself.
  2. On your new phone, tap Settings → Import and choose the file.

A backup contains everything, including private notes and game plans. Importing it replaces everything on that phone. boast shows how many opponents and matches each side has and asks before it does.

Settings

  • Name. Shown on the results you share.
  • Default format. Used for new opponents. After that, each opponent remembers the format you last played.
  • Appearance. Light (the default), Dark, or Match system.
  • Export backup and Import. See Two kinds of file.

Privacy

boast has no account, no ads and no tracking, and collects nothing. Data only leaves your phone when you choose to share or export it. Read the full privacy policy.
